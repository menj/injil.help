## Purpose and position

- The site answers the Islamic Dilemma: the claim that the Qur'an cannot affirm the earlier scripture and contradict it at once.
- The site's position: the Injīl is the revelation God gave to Jesus (5:46); the four gospels carry it and carry other things; the Qur'an is the measure (<em>muṣaddiq</em> and <em>muhaymin</em>, 5:48) that sorts the one from the other. Alteration of meaning and of text are each charged only where they can be shown.
- Every claim is labelled: scholarly consensus, minority view, or Islamic position. Gospel material is marked agrees / beyond the measure / unclear / conflicts / not needed.
- No placeholder text anywhere. Every page is complete when created.
- Laughter attributed to Jesus, docetism and mockery of the prophets in the Gnostic witnesses are set aside and the notes say so.
- No classical commentator names Simon of Cyrene as the substitute; the identification comes from Basilides through modern apologetics. Do not reintroduce the claim.
- No individual apologist is named on the secular-form page; the arguments are answered, not the people. The pillar page names the argument's historical proponents (Shamoun, Wood, Pfander, Ward) as its origin only. Pfander's Mīzān al-Ḥaqq is identified as the source of the Christian polemic and answered where it meets the site's premises on `/islamic-dilemma/balance-of-truth/`; it is quoted in short phrases only and never reproduced at length.
- The Gospel of Thomas in the Patterson and Meyer translation is reproduced with permission; Mattison's translations are public domain. Keep both credits on the sayings pages and the credits page.

## Journal

- The site has one blog, the Journal (owner's decision, 3 November 2026). Its posts are standard WordPress posts; no custom post type.
- The Journal page is the starter page `/journal/` with the template `page-journal` (a Query Loop of posts, newest first). `page_for_posts` and the front-page settings are not touched. The header menu and the footer's Reference list link to it.
- A post's address is `/journal/<slug>/` (`inc/journal.php`: rewrite rule and `post_link`). Slugs are words only, with no digits and no articles, like the pages.
- The posts are JSON in `inc/data/posts.json`: `slug`, `title`, `date` (`Y-m-d H:i:s`, site time), `category`, `excerpt`, `related` (Read next, starter-page paths) and `body` in the compact markup, ending in a `## Sources` list. Each is 700 to 1,100 words of prose with notes, at least two internal links, and a title tag that keeps the title rule (a short title under `journal/<slug>` in `seo-titles.json`).
- `injil_help_journal_seed()` writes them once per version (option `injil_help_journal_seeded`) and on activation, with the fixed `post_date`: a past date is `publish`, a future date `future`. A post is found again by its `_injil_help_post` meta, so a re-run writes nothing; a post edited by the owner (the page hashes) or binned is kept; a written post's date and status are never changed.
- The installer's sample post is still binned on activation (`injil_help_remove_sample_content()`).
- A post's breadcrumb is Home, Journal, the post; it is a `BlogPosting` in the page's `@graph`. Styles: `assets/css/journal.css`.

No slug carries verse numbers, a numeric prefix or a `-2` suffix. Every article carries a "Read next" row; a new page is added to `related.json` and linked from at least two existing pages.

## Starter content behaviour

- `injil_help_populate( $overwrite = false, $force = false )`. On activation: create missing pages, set pretty permalinks, bin the untouched sample post, page and comment, replace the default tagline.
- After an update (once per version, first admin load): rewrite pages whose text changed, skip pages the owner edited (post meta `_injil_help_hash` is missing or differs from the stored content, or `_injil_help_head` differs from the title and excerpt), keep no revisions, slash data, flush rewrite rules only when permalinks were set. "Update all pages from the theme" rewrites everything, edited pages included.
- Options written: `injil_help_populated` (version stamped only after a non-empty page list), `injil_help_populating` (lock, stale after two minutes), `injil_help_options`.

## Structured data and SEO

With the site name The Rightly-Guided Injīl that leaves 32 characters for the page part. Rank Math, Yoast and SEOPress get the same title, so the rule holds whichever writes the head. A new page with a title over 32 characters gets an entry in `seo-titles.json`.

## Contextual links

`inc/contextual-links.php` links the first mention of a known phrase in the running text of a single post or page (`is_singular( array( 'post', 'page' ) )`, so never an attachment; not the front page) to the inner page that treats it, via `the_content` at priority 15: after `do_blocks`, `wpautop`, `wptexturize` and shortcodes, before the share row (20). The guard matches the queried post rather than `in_the_loop()`, as the breadcrumb and share row do. This site's key is `injil` (`INJIL_HELP_SITE_KEY`).

- The map is `inc/data/network-links.json`: `sites` (key to base URL) and `links` (`site`, `path`, `phrases`). It is shared: the same file, byte for byte, ships in all seven themes (abrahamic, bestofislam, injil.help, paulus, compelling-evidence, murtadd, lailatulqadar). Change it in all seven or in none. Each phrase belongs to one page across the network, and each path must exist on the site that serves it.
- Entries for this site link with `home_url( path )`, so staging links to staging; entries for a sister site link to its base URL. Lines in `ctx_extra` come first and win; a full address there must be on a host in `sites`.
- Matching is whole phrase, capitals aside, curly apostrophes and non-breaking spaces allowed. The phrases are split into patterns of 150, because one pattern of the whole map is more than PCRE compiles; the text is scanned as one pattern would scan it: from the end of the last match, the earliest match of any pattern, the longest at one place. Links in navigation (the breadcrumb) do not count as links the text already makes.
- Never inside `a`, headings, `blockquote`, `q`, `figure`, `figcaption`, `cite`, `code`, `pre`, `sup`, `sub`, `nav`, `table`, form controls, `summary`, `aside`, `header`, `footer`, `lang` ar, he, el or syr, `dir="rtl"`, or the classes `ih-ar`, `ih-quran`, `ih-hadith`, `ih-verse`, `ih-v`, `ih-vq`, `ih-bible`, `ih-translit`, `ih-fnref`, `ih-footnotes`, `ih-foot-list`, `ih-apparatus`, `ih-lanah`, `ih-cite`, `ih-running-head`, `ih-chapter-head`, `ih-chapter-num`, `ih-eyebrow`, `ih-meta`, `ih-numeral`, `ih-mark`, `ih-breadcrumb`, `ih-share`, `ih-next`, `ih-entries-list`, `ih-answers-list`, `is-style-gospel-verse`.
- At most one new link in a `p` or `li`; each target once a page; never the page itself; a target the text already links to is skipped. Markup: `<a class="ih-ctx-link">` for this site, `<a class="ih-ctx-link ih-ctx-link--sister" rel="noopener">` for a sister site, same tab. The links take the theme's own link style; no CSS is added.
