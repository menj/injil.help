@chapter 6 Arrest, Trial and Aftermath
@section 25
Then Jesus got up, and he and his disciples went out to the mount of Olives, but Judas Iscariot did not go with them. And Jesus said unto them, this night ye will be all scattered. But after that I am arisen in the morning, I will go to Galilee before you[^199] Then they came to a place called Gethsemaine, and Jesus began to be greatly astonished and to be distressed: for he knew that Judas Iscariot had gone to betray him.[^200] And he saith to his disciples, Sit ye here, while I shall pray. And he taketh with him Peter and James and John, and saith unto them, My soul is grieved unto death: tarry ye here, and watch.
And he went forward a little, and fell on the ground, and prayed that if it were possible, the hour might pass from him.[^201] And he remained in prayer for an hour, and when he cometh unto the three disciples, he findeth them sleeping, and saith unto Peter, Simon, sleepest thou? Couldest not thou watch one hour? And again he went away, and prayed that the plots of the faithless against his life be defeated. And when he returned, he found them asleep again (for their eyes were heavy) neither wist they what to answer him.
Then he heard some men, and he saith unto his disciples, Arise, let us go hence; behold, he that betrayeth me hath drawn near. But while he yet spake, cometh Judas, one of the twelve, and with him a number of men with swords and staves, from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders. And as soon as they were come, Judas goeth straightway to him, and saith, Teacher, and then kissed him fervently. And by this they knew the man they had come to take, and they laid their hands on Jesus, and took him. And one of them that stood by (some say it was Peter) drew a sword, but when he saw the multitude of men with swords and staves, he and the other disciples fled.[^202]
And they led Jesus away to the high priest. And Simon Peter went to the house of a disciple who was known unto the high priest, and taking him goes to the palace of the high priest.[^203] And while Peter stood at the door without, the other disciple went into the palace, and saw Jesus before the high priest and the elders and the scribes and chief priests. Then he went out, and spoke unto her that kept the door, and brought in Peter. And the servants and officers stood there, who had made a fire of coals; for it was cold: and they warmed themselves: and Peter came and stood with them, and warmed himself.
Then saith the damsel that kept the door unto Peter, Art thou of this man’s disciples.[^204] He saith, I am not. But the maid began to say to them that stood by, This is one of them. And he denied it again. But they that stood by said unto him, Surely thou art one of them: for thou art a Galilean, and thy speech agreeth thereto. But he began to curse and to swear, saying, I know not this man of whom ye speak.
### Conspiracy Against Jesus
Remember when God said, “O Jesus! I will take you[^205] and raise you up to Myself. I will deliver you from those who disbelieve, and elevate your followers above the disbelievers until the Day of Judgment. Then to Me you will all return, and I will settle all your disputes. As for those who disbelieve, I will subject them to a severe punishment in this life and the Hereafter, and they will have no helpers. And as for those who believe and do good, they will be rewarded in full. And God does not like the wrongdoers.” We recite all this to you O Prophet as one of the signs[^206] and as a wise reminder.[^207]
@section 26
And the chief priests and all the council sought evidence against Jesus to put him to death; and found none. For many bare false witness against him, but their witness agreed not together. And the high priest stood up in the midst, and asked Jesus, saying, Answerest thou nothing? What is it which these witness against thee? But he held his peace, and answered nothing. Again the high priest asked him, Art thou the Messiah? And Jesus said, Thou sayest it. Nevertheless, the One Living God has annointed me to be His messenger to the children of Israel. If they would have received me, He would have given to them a kingdom like He gave unto David.
But ye had indeed rejected faith: so pursue your plans, and God will execute His plan; and He is the best of planners. Then some of those in the council rose up, and said, What need we any further evidence? Ye have heard that he setteth himself up as a King and the Messiah, and he did earlier enter into Jerusalem on the back of a colt, pretending to be the King of the Jews. Deliver him therefore to Pilate that he may crucify him for setting himself up as a King besides Caesar.[^208] And in the morning they brought him before Pontius Pilate, the governor, and said, We found this fellow perverting the nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, saying that he himself is the Messiah, a king.
And Pilate asked him, Knowest thou what thy people witness against thee? Art thou the King of the Jews.[^209] Jesus answered, My kingdom is not like thy kingdom: if my kingdom was like thine, then would I have soldiers to fight that I should not be delivered to thee by my people: but my kingdom is not like thine. Pilate therefore said unto him, Art thou a king of thy people then? And Jesus answering said unto him, How wouldst thou call a messenger to his people from the King of kings? Then the Jews who brought Jesus to Pilate said unto him, He hath said it; and thou knowest that whosoever maketh himself a king speaketh against Caesar.
Then Pilate brought Jesus forth, and sat down in the judgement seat in a place that is called the Pavement, but in the Hebrew, Gabbatha.[^210] And he pronounced, I sentence thee: thou shall go upon the cross. Lictor, bind his hands. Let him be flogged! And when he had been flogged, they took him away that they might crucify him at some time.[^211] Now at that feast Pilate wanted to release one prisoner from among the Jews[^212] that he might be more popular with them. And he chose Jesus Barabbas (meaning Jesus, son of a rabbi),[^213] a notable prisoner[^214] who had made an insurrection.
And Pilate sent an order, that Jesus Barabbas be released. But the officers who received the order did make an error, and released Jesus of Nazareth, and crucified Jesus Barabbas.[^215] And when he was released, he departed for Galilee, and he met two travellers who were going to Emmaus, which was from Jerusalem about three score furlong.[^216] And they talked together of all these things which had happened. And Jesus went with them, but their eyes were holden that they should not recognize him. And he said unto them, What words are these that ye exchange with each other walking?
And one of them, whose name was Cleopas, answering said unto him, Art thou only a stranger in Jerusalem, and hast not known the things which are come to pass there in these days? And he said unto them, What things? And they said unto him, Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, which was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people: And how the chief priests and our rulers delivered him to be crucified. And Jesus said unto them, Exchange ye words about him, or would ye have done aught for him? And they said, What could we have done?
And Jesus answering said unto them, Lo, Jesus of Nazareth is not crucified nor dead, but he liveth. Go back to Jerusalem tomorrow and tell his disciples that he goeth to Galilee, and there he shall see them. And then they came to the village, wither they went: and he made as though he would have gone further. But they constrained him, saying, Abide with us: for it is toward evening, and the day is far spent, and thou doest not look well. And he went in to tarry with them. And the next day he departed from thence and went further.
### Jesus Was Neither Killed nor Crucified
And for boasting, “We killed the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, the messenger of God.” But they neither killed nor crucified him—it was only made to appear so.[^217] Even those who argue for this crucifixion are in doubt. They have no knowledge whatsoever—only making assumptions. They certainly did not kill him. Rather, God raised him up to Himself. And God is Almighty, All-Wise.[^218]
@section 27
And a disciple of Jesus had prepared a tomb, hewn out in a rock, for the body of Jesus to be buried. And he went to the place, wither they took Jesus to crucify, and asked for his body. And the centurion said unto him, They have already taken his body: For the centurion thought that he had come for the body of Jesus Barabbas. And then the disciple goeth to the sepulchre, and rolled the stone, but he found nothing. And the next day in the morning Mary Magdalene and other women came to the tomb with sweet spices that they might annoint the body of Jesus.
And when they came unto the sepulchre, they found the stone rolled away, and the body of Jesus was not there. And they were greatly affrighted. Now as these things were happening, Jesus journeyed on the way to Galilee. And he met some of the people who had believed in him when he was preaching, and he told them what he had told to those which went to Emmaus. And it came to pass that Jesus fell sick on the way, and could not travel any further.[^219] And he tarried with some of his disciples in a village, and told them who he was and what had happened to him.[^220]
And they administered him, and they sent word to other disciples, whosoever they could find, that Jesus was not dead, but liveth. But Jesus’ condition continued to worsen, and after a few days God took him[^221] to Himself. May peace be upon Jesus the day he was born, and the day he died, and the day he will be raised to life again. And there went around divers sayings about Jesus: It was said, He is not dead but liveth. Some said, He was not crucified, and others, He was crucified and buried but he rose from the dead.[^222]
But God did not make some of the sayings that went around among the disciples of Jesus save a source of hope for the believers, and a means of honouring His messenger. Thus doth God help and honour His messengers, and createth for His righteous servants a way when there appeareth to be no way.[^223] For God is full of mercy and grace for men, but most of them are ever prone to reject faith, and they make their own gods besides Him.
### God’s Favour upon Jesus
And on Judgment Day God will say, “O Jesus, son of Mary! Remember My favour upon you and your mother: how I supported you with the holy spirit[^224] so you spoke to people in your infancy and adulthood. How I taught you writing, wisdom, the Torah, and the Gospel. How you healed the blind and the lepers—by My Will. How you brought the dead to life—by My Will. How I prevented the Children of Israel from harming you when you came to them with clear proofs and the disbelievers among them said, “This is nothing but pure magic.” And how I inspired the disciples, “Believe in Me and My messenger!” They declared, “We believe and bear witness that we fully submit to God.”[^225][^226]
### Jesus Denying Claim to Divinity
And on Judgment Day God will say, “O Jesus, son of Mary! Did you ever ask the people to worship you and your mother as gods besides God?” He will answer, “Glory be to You! How could I ever say what I had no right to say? If I had said such a thing, you would have certainly known it. You know what is hidden within me, but I do not know what is within You. Indeed, You alone are the Knower of all unseen. I never told them anything except what You ordered me to say: “Worship God—my Lord and your Lord!” And I was witness over them as long as I remained among them. But when You took me,[^227] You were the Witness over them—and You are a Witness over all things. If You punish them, they belong to You after all.[^228] But if You forgive them, You are surely the Almighty, All-Wise.” God will declare, “This is the Day when only the faithful will benefit from their faithfulness. Theirs are Gardens under which rivers flow, to stay there for ever and ever. God is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him. That is the ultimate triumph.” To God alone belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth and everything within. And He is Most Capable of everything.[^229]
## Closing
### The Qur’an Is a Confirmation
It is not possible for this Quran to have been produced by anyone other than God. In fact, it is a confirmation of what came before, and an explanation of the Scripture. It is, without a doubt, from the Lord of all worlds. Or do they claim, “He[^230] made it up!”? Tell them O Prophet, “Produce one chapter like it then, and seek help from whoever you can—other than God—if what you say is true!”[^231][^232]
### God’s Absolute Oneness
Say, O Prophet, “He is God, One and Indivisible; God—the Sustainer, needed by all.”[^233]
### One True God
God! There is no god worthy of worship except Him, the Ever-Living, All-Sustaining. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Who could possibly intercede with Him without His permission? He fully knows what is ahead of them and what is behind them, but no one can grasp any of His knowledge—except what He wills to reveal. His Seat encompasses the heavens and the earth, and the preservation of both does not tire Him. For He is the Most High, the Greatest.[^234]
### God’s Beautiful Names
“He is God—there is no god worthy of worship except Him: Knower of the seen and unseen. He is the Most Compassionate, Most Merciful. He is God—there is no god except Him: the King, the Most Holy, the All-Perfect, the Source of Serenity, the Watcher of all, the Almighty, the Supreme in Might, the Majestic. Glorified is God far above what they associate with Him in worship! He is God: the Creator, the Inventor, the Shaper. He alone has the Most Beautiful Names. Whatever is in the heavens and the earth constantly glorifies Him. And He is the Almighty, All-Wise.”[^235]
