@chapter 2 John and the Beginning of the Ministry
@section 5
And John, the son of Zacharias, too, was given wisdom and good judgement even as a youth: He grew up in the wilderness to be tender to all creatures; and he was pure and devout; Kind to his parents; and he was not overbearing or rebellious.[^51] He renounced the pleasures of this world and preferred garments of camel’s hair over soft raiment; and he did eat locusts and wild honey.[^52] And in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, when Pontius Pilate was governor of Judea, and Herod Antipas was the Tetrarch of Galilee, his brother Philip the Tetrarch of Ituraea and of the region of Trachonitis, and Lysanias the Tetrarch of Abilene; and when Caiaphas and his father-in-law were the high priests, A word from God came unto John in the wilderness, and he began to preach in the country about the river Jordan, Saying, Repent and reform for the day of the Lord is drawing near.
And he administered the baptism of repentance for the remission of sins. Then went out to him whole Jerusalem and all Judea and all the region round about Jordan, To be baptized of him in Jordan and to confess their sins. And baptizing them, he said, O generation of vipers, who hath warned you to flee from the wrath to come? Bring forth, therefore, fruits worthy of repentance, and begin not to say that we have Abraham to our father: for I say unto you that God is able of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham.
And now also the axe is laid unto the roots of the trees: every tree, therefore, which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down and cast into the fire. And the people asked him, saying, What shall we do then? He answered and saith unto them, He that hath two coats, let him impart one to him that hath none; and he that hath meat, let him do likewise. And there also came tax collectors to be baptized and said unto him, Master, what shall we do? And he said unto them, Exact no more than that which is appointed to you.
And soldiers likewise asked him, saying, And what shall we do? And he said unto them, Do violence to no man, neither accuse any falsely, and be content with your wages.[^53] And as the people were in expectation, all men mused in their hearts of John whether he was the Messiah of Aaron, or the Messiah of Israel, or that Prophet[^54] John answered, saying unto them all, I indeed am annointed to be the priest of the house of Aaron, who was to come in the last days, And from among my disciples whom I have baptized there shall arise the Messiah of Israel.
And as for that prophet, of whom God has been promising through the mouths of all his messengers, he will come after us to be a light for the nations.
@section 6
And Jesus was approaching forty,[^55] and he came from Galilee to Jordan unto John to be baptized of him. And when he was baptized, he went up out of the water, and, lo, the heavens were opened unto him, and he saw the Holy Spirit descending like a dove and lighting upon him, And he heard a voice from heaven,[^56] saying, Thou art of the three I have chosen.[^57] Then Jesus knew that this was the hour of his appointment[^58] as a messenger of God to Israel. That he may support and continue John’s work[^59] of reform of Israel and prepare the way for the light which will lighten the nations.
But in his mind the devil started to put doubts about this.[^60] And Jesus, perplexed, retired in the wilderness to be by himself; but the devil followed him. And when Jesus hungered, the devil said, If thou be a chosen of God, command this stone that it be made bread. But Jesus answered unto him, It is written that man shall not live by bread alone but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God. Then the devil saith unto him, If thou be a chosen of God, go to Jerusalem, set thyself on the pinnacle of the temple,[^61] and cast thyself down; for it is written, He shall give his angels charge over thee to keep thee, And in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.
And Jesus, answering, said unto him, It is said, Thou shalt not test the Lord thy God. And then the devil showed him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory and said unto him, All this power and glory I will give thee. If thou, therefore, wilt worship me, all shall be thine. And Jesus answered and said unto him, Get thee behind me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and Him only shalt thou serve. With the power of these words, the devil went away. And Jesus came to John and told him what happened to him, and John witnessed the truth of the voice from heaven.[^62] And he said unto him, Wait for the word of God.
And Jesus wandered with John in Judea and they went as far as Aenon near Salim.[^63] And John, fearing no one but God, boldly reproved Herod the Tetrarch of Galilee for marrying Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife, Saying, it is not lawful for thee to have thy brother’s wife. And for many other things did John reprove Herod, and he also reproved Herodias for adultery. But Herod Antipas added yet this above all his evils, that he put John in prison.[^64] Now after that John was put in prison, Jesus came into Galilee,[^65] and tarried in Nazareth, where he was brought up.[^66]
And on a sabbath day, as his custom was, he went to the synagogue. And the attendant stood up with the Scriptures waiting for someone to come and read a passage and explain it. And that sabbath day it was Jesus who stood up to read. And there was delivered unto him the book of the prophet Esias; and when he opened the book, he found the place where it was written, The spirit of the Lord is upon me, because the Lord hath annointed me to preach the good news unto the meek; he hath sent me to bind the broken-hearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives.
To proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord and the day of vengeance of our God. And having read the passage, he closed the book and gave it back to the attendant and sat down; and the eyes of all those that were in the synagogue were fastened on him.[^67] And he stood up again and began to say unto them, This day is this Scripture fulfilled in your ears. When people heard this, they said one to another, Is not this the carpenter’s son? Is not his mother called Mary? and his brothers James, and Joses, and Simon, and Judas: And are not his sisters here with us?
And they were offended that one among them should speak of himself as a prophet of God. But Jesus said unto them, Verily I say unto you, no prophet is accepted in his own city. But I tell you of a truth, many widows were in Israel in the days of Elias,[^68] when the heaven was shut up three years and six months, when great famine was throughout all the land; But unto none of them was Elias sent, save unto a widow of Sarepta, a city of Sidon. And many lepers there were in Israel in the city of Eliseus the prophet, and none of them was cleansed, saving Naaman the Syrian.
And all they in the synagogue, when they heard these things, were filled with wrath, And rose up, and thrust him out of the city, and led him into the brow of the hill whereon the city was built, that they might cast him down headlong.[^69] But he escaped them and went his way; and leaving Nazareth he went towards Capernaum,[^70] a city of Galilee. Preaching on the way, Repent, for the day of the Lord is drawing near.[^71]
@section 7
And there were in Capernaum many disciples of John the Baptist,[^72] and two of them were Andrew and his brother Simon. And they took Jesus to their house,[^73] and there he found that the mother of Simon’s wife was sick of a fever. And Jesus stood over her, took her hand, and prayed to the Lord, And the fever left her and she arose.[^74] And on the sabbath day Jesus went to the synagogue and began to preach to the people there, Saying, O children of Israel, fear the day of the Lord when your father Abraham will be no wise able to save you, Say not therefore within yourself, We have Abraham to our father: for, as John said to you, God is able of stones to raise up children unto Abraham.
Neither is salvation by the law of Moses alone, but by faith in the One True Living God, Who is my Creator and your Creator, my Lord and your Lord; so serve Him and Him alone. This is the way that is straight.[^75] As Jesus was speaking these words, there was a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out, Saying, Let us alone; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? Art thou come to destroy us? And Jesus said unto the spirit, By the permission of the One True Living God, I command thee to come out of him.
And when the spirit had torn him and cried with a loud voice, it came out of him. And the people there were amazed,[^76] but some were offended because of what he said about Abraham and the Law of Moses, even though he had said nothing against them. But those who admired him took his fame into every place of the country round about. And at even, when the sun was set, people brought unto him all that were diseased and that were possessed with demons. And a multitude gathered together at the door of the house of Andrew and Simon.
And he laid his hands on every one of those that were diseased and those that were possessed with demons, and as many as God willed were healed.[^77] And one morning, rising up a great while before day, he went out and departed into a solitary place, and there prayed. And Simon and Andrew and other people followed after him. And when they had found him, they said unto him, All men seek thee. And he said unto them, Let us go in the next towns, that I may preach there, also; for therefore came I away. And he preached in their synagogues throughout all Galilee and cast out devils.[^78]
And the scribes and the rulers of the synagogue began to be offended by him because of what he preached and because the people admired him for the healings.
@section 8
And it came to pass that there came a leper, beseeching him, and saying unto him, If thou art willing, thou canst make me clean. And Jesus, moved with compassion, put forth his hand and touched him and said unto him, Be thou clean! Then, strongly admonishing him, immediately he sent him away, Saying, See thou say nothing to any man, but go and show thyself to the priest, and offer for thy cleansing those things which Moses commanded.[^79] And it came to pass on the second sabbath of the first month, that Jesus and his disciples passed through the corn fields; and his disciples plucked ears of corn and did eat, rubbing them in their hands.
And certain of the Pharisees saw them and said unto them, Why do ye that which is not lawful to do on the sabbath days? And Jesus, answering them, said, Have ye not read so much as this, what David did, when himself was an hungered, and they which were with him, How he went into the house of God in the days of Abiathar, the high priest, and did take and eat the shew bread, and gave also to them that were with him, which it is not lawful to eat but for the priests? And he said unto them, The sabbath was made for man, and not man for sabbath; therefore a man is lord of the sabbath and not sabbath the lord of man.[^80]
And Jesus began to preach out of the synagogues to the poor and to the publicans and sinners.[^81] And one day he went up a mount, and his disciples were gathered around him and a multitude with them. And he began to preach, Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted. Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth. Blessed are they that do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled. Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.
Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God. Blessed are the peace makers: for they shall be called the children of God. Blessed are they that are persecuted for righteousness sake: for great is their reward in heaven.[^82] And many other things did he preach, and when he was finished, the people were astonished at his doctrine. For he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.[^83]
